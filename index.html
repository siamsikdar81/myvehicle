Backup & restore

Save all your documents, reminders, fuel records and photo in one file. Keep it in Google Drive or send it to yourself. Restore it on a new phone or after reinstalling.

My V-Doc

Add a photo from your phone, take a picture with the camera, or save a scanned copy. Files are stored in this browser on this device.

Place the paper on a flat, bright surface, keep all four corners visible, take a clear photo, then save it here. For a PDF scan, choose the PDF file from your phone.

Note: saved files stay on this device/browser. Export or back up important originals separately. Very large files may exceed browser storage limits.

Service Alert

Set a reminder for an oil change, servicing, registration renewal or another task.

When the app is open, the reminder rings, vibrates and shows a notification at the set time. To be alerted when the app is closed, tap “Add to calendar alarm” on a reminder (your phone calendar will ring).
